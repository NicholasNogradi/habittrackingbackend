---
title: "Categories"
description: "Group habits into user-scoped categories."
---

Categories are optional, user-owned labels that group related habits (e.g., "Health", "Learning"). Each habit references at most one category.

## Operations

- `listCategories` — paginated list of the caller's categories.
- `createCategory` — create a new category. Honors `Idempotency-Key`.
- `getCategory` — read a single category by ID.
- `updateCategory` — rename or recolor a category (`If-Match` required).
- `deleteCategory` — remove a category. Habits pointing at it are unlinked, not deleted.

See the **API Reference** tab for exact fields and constraints.

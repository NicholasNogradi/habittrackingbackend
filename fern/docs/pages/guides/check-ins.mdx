---
title: "Check-ins"
description: "Record completion events against a habit."
---

A **check-in** is a single completion record for a habit — the raw data streaks and analytics are computed from. Check-ins are always nested under a habit at `/habits/{habitId}/checkins`.

## Operations

- `listCheckins` — paginated. Filter by `from`, `to`, and `status`.
- `createCheckin` — record a completion. Honors `Idempotency-Key`.
- `getCheckin` — read a single check-in.
- `updateCheckin` — amend status or notes (`If-Match` required).
- `deleteCheckin` — remove a check-in (`If-Match` required).

<Callout intent="info">
Deleting or amending past check-ins retroactively recomputes affected streaks. See [Streaks](/guides/streaks).
</Callout>

---
title: "Habits"
description: "Create and manage the trackable behaviors at the core of the API."
---

A **habit** is the recurring behavior a user wants to track — e.g., "Meditate", "Run 5km". Habits carry a `frequency` (`daily`, `weekly`, or `custom`), an optional `categoryId`, and a `status` (`active`, `paused`, `archived`).

## Operations

- `listHabits` — paginated. Filter by `categoryId`, `status`, `frequency`; sort with `sort`.
- `createHabit` — create a habit. Honors `Idempotency-Key`.
- `getHabit` — read a single habit by ID.
- `replaceHabit` — full `PUT` replacement (`If-Match` required).
- `updateHabit` — partial `PATCH` (`If-Match` required).
- `deleteHabit` — cascade-deletes check-ins, streaks, and reminders.

Habits are the parent for check-ins, streaks, and reminders — those live under `/habits/{habitId}/…`.

---
title: "Analytics"
description: "Aggregate summaries and per-habit trends."
---

Analytics endpoints return pre-aggregated views over a user's check-in history. They are read-only.

## Operations

- `getAnalyticsSummary` — cross-habit totals for a date range (`from`, `to`).
- `getHabitTrends` — per-habit time series for a range at a `granularity` of `day`, `week`, or `month`.

<Callout intent="info">
Analytics reflect the current state of check-ins at query time — editing a past [check-in](/guides/check-ins) changes future summary responses.
</Callout>

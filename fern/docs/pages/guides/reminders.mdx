---
title: "Reminders"
description: "Scheduled nudges attached to a habit."
---

Reminders are scheduled prompts that ask a user to check in on a habit. They are nested under a habit at `/habits/{habitId}/reminders` — a reminder cannot exist without a parent habit.

## Operations

- `listReminders` — list reminders for a habit.
- `createReminder` — schedule a new reminder. Honors `Idempotency-Key`.
- `getReminder` — read a single reminder.
- `updateReminder` — change time, cadence, or channel (`If-Match` required).
- `deleteReminder` — cancel a reminder (`If-Match` required).

See the **API Reference** tab for the reminder schema (time, day-of-week mask, delivery channel).
